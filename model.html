English programmes for organisations

English for international work and solidarity.

We run online English programmes for organisations and specialise in activism and solidarity work. Your staff or members can learn together in a group or have private lessons, focusing on advocacy and organising, or on business English and professional communication.

Start with a free consultation. Tell us how people in your organisation use English and what they’d like to improve, and the teacher who’d lead your programme will send you a written proposal.

lightbulbWhat could your group work on?

handshakeMeetings and international partnerships

Help your team take a full part in meetings with international partners. Participants will practise how to talk about their work, respond to colleagues and ask for clarification when a discussion becomes hard to follow.

co_presentPresentations and public speaking

Prepare for a talk or public presentation. Participants will plan how to get their ideas across, rehearse their delivery and practise answering questions.

descriptionReports, emails and briefings

Improve your team’s written English. Participants might rework a report to make the argument easier to follow, or practise writing emails in the right tone. They can bring examples from their own work.

campaignAdvocacy and campaign work

In our specialist classes, activists learn to explain their campaigns to a wider audience. They might prepare a briefing for international partners or rehearse what they’ll say at a campaign meeting, then practise answering questions about the issues.

chatGeneral English for staff or members

For people who’d like to improve their English more generally, we offer regular speaking and listening practice. We can add reading and writing to suit the group.

routeHow it works

  1. forum1. Talk to a lead teacher

    In a free consultation, tell a lead teacher about the people taking part and how they use English. If there’s a particular task coming up, bring it along.

  2. rate_review2. Review the proposal

    Your lead teacher will then send you a written proposal. You’ll see what the programme will cover, the total fee, the payment schedule and the cancellation terms before you agree to anything.

  3. play_circle3. Start the programme

    You bring the group together, and your lead teacher will usually design and teach the programme, drawing on examples from your own work.

  4. tune4. Adjust as you go

    Your teacher will adapt later sessions to the group’s progress and feedback. We’ll agree any bigger change to the scope or number of sessions with you first, including any change to the fee.

Example programme

publicEnglish for international meetings

Here’s how a group might prepare for meetings with international partners over four 55-minute sessions. For six participants, it would cost £240 in total (£10 per person per session).

Who it’s for

Six staff or volunteers who regularly meet partner organisations.

Aim

To speak up in meetings and explain current projects clearly.

  1. Session 1

    Introduce your organisation and explain its current work.

  2. Session 2

    Give a project update and respond to what colleagues say.

  3. Session 3

    Ask follow-up questions and check you’ve understood other speakers.

  4. Session 4

    Bring it all together in a practice meeting, then get feedback from your teacher.

diversity_3Who we work with

We welcome enquiries from organisations doing solidarity and public-interest work, from trade unions and campaign groups to charities and community organisations.

personOne lead teacher from start to finish

You’ll usually work with the same lead teacher from the first consultation to the final session. You’ll always know who to talk to, and they’ll get to know your group as the programme goes on.

helpFrequently asked questions

How many people can take part?

Group sessions are for four or more learners, and you’ll agree the maximum group size with your lead teacher during the consultation. Individual staff or members can also have private lessons.

Can you work with different English levels?

Yes, as long as everyone can take part in the planned activities. Your lead teacher will help you decide whether people should learn together, in separate groups or in private lessons.

How long does a programme last?

That depends on your group’s goals and the time you have. Your teacher will recommend a course length during the consultation.

Can we use examples from our own work?

Yes. Just agree suitable material with your lead teacher first. Anything confidential will be seen only by your lead teacher and the colleagues at Najma who need it to coordinate your programme.

What happens if we need to change or cancel a session?

You’ll find the terms for cancelling and rescheduling in your proposal, so you can check them before you agree to the programme.
