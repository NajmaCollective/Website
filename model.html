About Najma

English programmes for organisations

English for international work and solidarity.

Najma offers online English teaching for organisations, with a particular focus on activism and solidarity work. We teach English for advocacy and organising alongside business English and professional communication skills. Your staff or members can learn together as a group or take private lessons.

Start with a free consultation to discuss how your organisation uses English and what participants would like to improve. The teacher who would lead the programme will then prepare a written proposal for you to review.

lightbulbWhat could your group work on?

handshakeMeetings and international partnerships

Help your team take part in meetings with international partners. Participants can practise explaining their work and responding to colleagues, with time to explore how to ask for clarification when a discussion becomes difficult to follow.

co_presentPresentations and public speaking

Prepare for an upcoming talk or public presentation with a teacher’s guidance. Participants can work on how to explain their ideas to an audience, then rehearse their delivery and practise answering questions.

descriptionReports, emails and briefings

Work on the English your organisation uses in writing. Participants might revise a report so that its argument is easier to follow or practise writing emails with an appropriate tone. Lessons can use examples from their own work.

campaignAdvocacy and campaign work

Specialist English classes can help activists explain their campaigns to a wider audience. Participants might prepare a briefing for international partners or rehearse a contribution to a campaign meeting. The teaching focuses on expressing the issues clearly and responding to questions about them.

chatGeneral English for staff or members

For staff or members who would like to improve their English more generally, a programme can provide regular speaking and listening practice. Reading and writing can also form part of the teaching, according to the group’s needs.

routeHow it works

  1. forum1. Talk to a lead teacher

    Tell the lead teacher who will take part and how people use English in your organisation. If there is a particular task you want to prepare for, you can discuss it during the consultation.

  2. rate_review2. Review the proposal

    Your written proposal explains what the programme will cover and how the sessions will be delivered. It also confirms the total fee and payment schedule, together with the cancellation terms, so you can review the arrangement before approving it.

  3. play_circle3. Start the programme

    Your organisation brings together the participants, while the lead teacher usually designs and teaches the programme. Suitable examples from your work can help make the lessons relevant to the situations people encounter.

  4. tune4. Adjust later sessions

    As the programme develops, the teacher can adjust later sessions in response to participants’ progress and feedback. Any larger change to the scope or number of sessions will be agreed with you first, including any effect on the fee.

Example programme

publicEnglish for international meetings

This example shows how a group could prepare for meetings with international partners over four 55-minute sessions. For six participants, the teaching would cost £240 in total at £10 per person per session.

Who it is for

Six staff or volunteers who regularly join meetings with partner organisations.

Aim

Speak in meetings and explain current projects clearly.

Session 1

Introduce the organisation and explain current work.

Session 2

Practise giving a project update and responding to colleagues’ contributions.

Session 3

Practise asking follow-up questions and checking that you have understood other speakers.

Session 4

Bring the earlier work together in a practice meeting and receive feedback from the teacher.

diversity_3Who we work with

We welcome enquiries from organisations involved in solidarity and public-interest work. This includes trade unions and campaign groups, as well as charities and community organisations. We can discuss specialist advocacy classes or English teaching for your organisation’s wider professional work.

personOne lead teacher from start to finish

Your lead teacher will usually stay with the programme from the initial consultation through to the final session. This gives you a consistent point of contact and allows the teaching to build on what participants have already covered.

sellPricing

pie_chartHow Najma’s model works

About Najma

helpFrequently asked questions

How much does a programme cost?

Group sessions cost £10 per learner for 55 minutes, with a minimum of four learners per class. Private lessons cost £20 per learner for 55 minutes. Your proposal confirms the number of sessions and total programme fee before teaching begins.

How many people can take part?

Organisational group classes have a minimum of four learners. Your organisation supplies the group, and the lead teacher agrees the maximum class size with you during the consultation. Private lessons are also available for individual staff or members.

Can you work with different English levels?

A group can include different levels of English when everyone can take part in the planned activities. Your lead teacher will help you decide whether people would benefit from learning together or from separate groups or private lessons.

How long does a programme last?

The number of sessions depends on the group’s goals and the time available. Your teacher will recommend a programme length during the consultation, and the proposal will confirm the arrangement.

Can we use examples from our own work?

Yes. You can agree suitable material with the lead teacher so that the lessons relate to your organisation’s work. Access to confidential information is limited to the lead teacher and the Najma role-holders who need it for coordination.

What happens if we need to change or cancel a session?

The proposal sets out the cancellation and rescheduling terms. You can review these before approving the programme.
