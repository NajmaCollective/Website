About Najma

Najma Collective

Online English lessons with a teacher you choose

Learn English one-to-one with an experienced teacher, at a time that suits you. Study regularly to build your English over time, or book a single lesson to prepare for an interview, a presentation or an important email.

We’re a collective of English teachers, built on solidarity and fair pay for the people who teach. We also run programmes for organisations and host a free Solidarity Café, where people meet to talk about social change.

Private lessons: £20 for 55 minutes.

For organisations: arrange a free consultation

local_cafeMeet Najma at the Solidarity Café

The Solidarity Café is an informal online conversation about solidarity and social change. A facilitator keeps the discussion moving, and you’re welcome to join in or simply listen.

It’s free during our pilot, and sessions usually last 55–⁠75 minutes. Conversations are in English, so we recommend an intermediate level (around B1) or above. You don’t need to take lessons with us to come along.

Private lessons

£20 for 55 minutes.

Each lesson gives you 55 minutes of your teacher’s full attention. Work towards a long-term goal, or focus on something coming up soon, like a seminar or a meeting with colleagues. You book one lesson at a time, so you can keep learning with the same teacher for as long as it helps.

Some teachers also offer 25-minute lessons for £10. If you’d like to meet a teacher before you book, look for a free 20-minute introductory call on their profile.

timelineEvery lesson builds on the last

The longer you study with the same teacher, the better they understand how you learn and what you’re aiming for. They’ll bring back earlier work when it helps, and adjust the focus as your priorities change.

If you move to another Najma teacher, they can pick up where you left off.

corporate_fareEnglish for your organisation

We design English programmes for organisations, especially those working in activism and solidarity. A programme might help your team explain a campaign to international partners, or speak up with more confidence in meetings. We also teach business English and professional communication.

Everything starts with a free consultation. The teacher who would lead your programme will listen to what your organisation needs, then send you a written proposal to review.

Group sessions cost £10 per learner for 55 minutes, for groups of four or more. Private lessons cost £20 per learner for 55 minutes. Your proposal confirms the total fee and booking terms, and your lead teacher will normally design and teach the programme themselves.

diversity_3A collective run by its teachers

Every teacher at Najma has an equal say in how the collective is run, and each one decides how they teach. That leaves them free to choose the methods and materials that suit you.

During the pilot, your teacher receives 80% of what you pay.

The other 20% covers the work we share, such as bookings and the Solidarity Café, and a proposed 5% contribution to Al Manar Society. That contribution will begin once Al Manar has approved it.
