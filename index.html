A COLLECTIVE WITH A PURPOSE

Good teaching starts
with valued teachers.

Najma’s teachers have an equal say in how the collective runs. Our work connects English teaching with solidarity and fair conditions for teachers.

Revenue shares are calculated after refunds and before processing charges. The proposed 5% contribution to Al Manar begins once the arrangement is approved.
