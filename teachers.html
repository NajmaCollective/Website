Choosing a teacher

A teacher’s profile can help you decide whether their experience and approach fit what you are looking for. If you are preparing for an exam or working on academic writing, for example, look for relevant teaching experience in that area.

Continuity as you learn

Working with the same teacher over time allows your lessons to develop as they get to know you. You can revisit earlier learning and change the focus as your English improves.

If your schedule or priorities change, you can book another available Najma teacher. They can build on your previous lessons so your learning continues through the change.

Frequently asked questions

Do all Najma teachers offer the same lessons?

Each teacher’s profile explains their teaching areas and available private lesson options. Short lessons and free introductory appointments are available where the teacher offers them.

Do private lessons cost the same with every teacher?

Yes. Standard private lessons cost £20 for 55 minutes during the pilot. A 25-minute lesson costs £10 where a teacher offers it.

Can I book lessons with more than one teacher?

Yes. You can continue with one teacher or book another available teacher when you want a different focus or appointment time. Your new teacher can build on what you have already studied.

How do I know a teacher’s qualifications are accurate?

Najma checks the qualifications and relevant experience shown on each published profile. Where a qualification may be unfamiliar, the profile explains it in plain English.
