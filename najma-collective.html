    Continuity across teachers

    Your progress can travel with you.

    Regular learners can keep a short learning record in a shared folder. You control access. The record captures your goals, completed work and agreed next steps. If schedules change, another Najma teacher can pick up the thread without asking you to begin again.

    Why Najma exists

    Fair teaching can sustain free community education.

    Some teachers connected with Noor Community have taught voluntarily for more than a year. Their generosity has kept learning open under extraordinary pressure. It has also carried a human cost. Exhaustion enters the classroom. Preparation time shrinks. Learners can see the strain and may feel guilty for receiving support.

    Najma creates a separate stream of paid teaching for learners beyond the Palestinian community. It serves people in activist, solidarity, academic and professional spaces. Their fees create paid hours for expert teachers while free community education remains protected.

    People already choose fair-trade food and clothing made under decent conditions. Najma applies the same principle to English education. The learner receives serious teaching. The teacher receives fairer work. A Palestinian cultural organisation receives a steady contribution.

    For teachers

    Bring your expertise. Keep your professional voice.

    Najma begins with a small international group of highly qualified teachers. Each teacher chooses their methods, materials, meeting platform, rate and availability. Najma provides one shared place for learners to find you and book.

    The founding group will decide together how much shared infrastructure genuinely helps learners. Any common system should protect continuity while leaving teachers free to teach well.
