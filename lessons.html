What you can learn

A lesson begins with the outcome you need.

Najma teachers respond to the work in front of you. They use their professional judgement to choose the language, practice and feedback that will move you forward.

    task_alt

    A useful hour

    Bring something real. Leave with something usable.

    1. 1
      Set the outcome

      Tell the teacher what you need to do and who you need to communicate with.

    2. 2
      Work on the task

      Practise, redraft and test the language with focused expert feedback.

    3. 3
      Take the next step

      Finish with a usable version, clear notes and a plan for independent practice.

    folder_shared

    Continuity across teachers

    Your progress can travel with you.

    Regular learners can keep a short learning record in a shared folder. You control access. The record captures your goals, completed work and agreed next steps. If schedules change, another Najma teacher can pick up the thread without asking you to begin again.
