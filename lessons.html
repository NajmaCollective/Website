02 / Learn with Najma

What would you like to study?

Your teacher can help you study English more generally or concentrate on one of the areas below. Their profile explains the experience they bring to that work.

language General English

Improve your fluency through conversation about everyday life and topics that interest you. As you speak, your teacher can help you express your meaning more clearly and understand language that feels unfamiliar. Reading and writing can also form part of your lessons.

business_center Business and professional English

Practise communicating clearly in the situations you encounter at work. You could rehearse a presentation and the questions that follow it, or work with your teacher on the wording and tone of an email. Lessons can also provide regular practice for meetings and conversations with colleagues.

school Academic English

Develop the English you use in university study, with guidance on academic writing and discussion. Your teacher can help you organise an argument or explain your research, then give you opportunities to practise contributing to a seminar or presenting your work.

menu_book IELTS preparation

Prepare for IELTS speaking and writing through practice with the test tasks. Feedback linked to the assessment criteria will help you identify what to improve and return to it in later lessons. Each teacher’s profile states which test versions and components they cover.

campaign English for advocacy and organising

Work on the English you use in advocacy and organising, whether you are explaining an issue to partners or preparing to speak in public. You can develop a briefing or rehearse a campaign discussion with your teacher, paying attention to how your message will be understood by different audiences.

03 / Learn with Najma

How lessons develop

You and your teacher agree what to work on first. They may suggest an activity or material after hearing how you use English and what you would like to do more easily.

As you continue, later lessons can return to earlier work and introduce new language when it becomes useful. Your teacher can adjust the pace and focus as your priorities change.

04 / Learn with Najma

What your fee supports

Najma is run by its teachers. During the pilot, 80% of revenue from a paid lesson goes to the teacher who delivers it. The remaining 20% supports the collective’s shared roles and includes a proposed contribution to Al Manar.

The proposed 5% contribution to Al Manar and the public relationship begin once Al Manar approves the arrangement. Revenue shares are calculated after refunds and before payment-processing charges.

08 / Learn with Najma

How lessons connect over time

Regular lessons give your teacher time to understand how you learn and what you want to achieve. Together you can return to difficult areas and recognise the progress you make.

As your English develops, the focus of your lessons can change with it. You might spend several sessions on an area that needs attention or bring a new task when one arises.

If you choose another Najma teacher, they can build on what you have already studied so that your learning continues across the change.

How your lesson fee supports Najma

Najma keeps shared costs low so that private lessons can remain affordable. During the pilot, 80% of paid revenue is allocated to the teacher delivering the work. The remaining 20% funds shared roles and includes a proposed contribution to Al Manar.

Teachers retain professional control over their methods and materials. The About page explains the full allocation of fees and the proposed relationship with Al Manar.

09 / Learn with Najma

Learning with your organisation

Group sessions: £10 per learner for 55 minutes, with a minimum of four learners. Private lessons: £20 per learner for 55 minutes.

Najma offers specialist English teaching for advocacy and organising alongside business English and professional communication training. Your organisation supplies the participant group, or you can arrange private lessons for individual staff or members. After a free consultation, a lead teacher prepares a written proposal covering the programme and its booking terms.

Your proposal confirms the number of learners and sessions, together with the total programme fee, before teaching begins. Your lead teacher will normally design and deliver the programme.

11 / Learn with Najma

Practical questions

How do I book a lesson?

Choose a teacher, open their available appointments and select the lesson you want. The booking page will show the teacher’s name, the duration and the price before you pay. Your lesson is confirmed when payment is received.

Where do lessons take place?

Lessons take place online. Your booking confirmation tells you which meeting platform the teacher uses and gives you the joining link.

How can I check my English level?

The British Council’s free online level test gives an approximate guide to your English through grammar and vocabulary questions. You need a free LearnEnglish account to take it. Your teacher can discuss the result with you when you begin.

What happens if I need to cancel?

For private lessons, you can choose a full refund or one transfer to another available lesson if you give at least 24 hours’ notice. With less notice, the fee remains payable. Your teacher may offer extra flexibility in exceptional circumstances. If your teacher cancels, you can choose a full refund or a transfer.

Can I change teachers?

Yes. You can continue with the same teacher or book another available Najma teacher. Your new teacher can use your learning record to build on your earlier lessons.

Who can see my learning record?

Access is limited to people who need the record for teaching or learner support, including booking administration. Email info@najmacollective.org to ask about your information or request a correction or a restriction on access.

How can I ask a question or give feedback?

Email info@najmacollective.org for help with lessons or booking. You can use the same address to share feedback or raise a concern.
