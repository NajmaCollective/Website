chatGeneral English

Become more fluent by talking about everyday life and the things that interest you. As you talk, your teacher will help you say what you mean more clearly and make sense of new language. You can work on reading and writing too.

Practise communicating clearly in the situations you face at work. Rehearse a presentation and the questions that follow, or work on the wording and tone of an important email.

Prepare for IELTS Speaking and Writing with exam-style practice tasks. Your teacher will give you feedback based on the official assessment criteria, so you’ll know exactly what to improve.

Practise explaining an issue to partners or speaking in public. Prepare a briefing or rehearse a campaign discussion with your teacher, and think about how different audiences will hear your message.

trending_upHow lessons develop

First, you and your teacher agree what to work on. Once they’ve heard how you use English and what you’d like to find easier, they’ll suggest activities and materials to match.

As you go on, your teacher will revisit earlier work, introduce new language when you need it and adjust the pace as your priorities change. The more lessons you have together, the better they’ll understand how you learn.

Najma is run by its teachers, and we keep shared costs low so that lessons stay affordable.

During the pilot, your teacher receives 85% of what you pay.

The other 15% covers shared work, such as bookings and the Solidarity Café, and includes a proposed 5% contribution to Al Manar Society for Culture & Creativity. That contribution will begin once Al Manar has approved it.

helpFrequently asked questions

How do I book a lesson?

Choose a teacher, open their calendar and pick a time. Before you pay, you’ll see your teacher’s name, the length of the lesson and the price. Your lesson is confirmed once your payment has gone through.

Where do lessons take place?

Online, on the video platform your teacher uses. You’ll find the link to join in your booking confirmation.

What happens if I need to cancel?

For private lessons, it depends on how much notice you give.

  • At least 24 hours before your lesson: you can have a full refund, or move the lesson to another available time. You can move a lesson once.
  • Less than 24 hours before: you’ll still need to pay for the lesson, though your teacher may be flexible in exceptional circumstances.
  • If your teacher cancels: you can choose a full refund or a new time.

Can I change teachers?

Yes. You can stay with the same teacher for as long as you like, or book any other available Najma teacher. Your new teacher can use your learning record to pick up where you left off.

Who can see my learning record?

Only the people who need it to teach you, support your learning or manage your bookings. To ask what information we hold, or to request a correction or a limit on who can see it, email info@najmacollective.org.

How can I ask a question or give feedback?

Email info@najmacollective.org. We’re happy to help with lessons and bookings, and we want to hear your feedback, including any concerns.
