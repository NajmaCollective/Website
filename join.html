A SPACE FOR CONVERSATION

Solidarity Café

Free online conversations about solidarity and social change.

The Solidarity Café brings together adults who care about solidarity and social change. You are welcome whether you already take part in activism or community work, or would like to meet people and explore these interests.

Sessions offer time to talk about issues that matter to the group, with a facilitator helping the conversation along. We use English as our shared language, and you are welcome to listen as well as speak.

Free during the pilot · Online · 55–75 minutes · B1 or above recommended

NAJMA / 02

What happens in a session?

A facilitator hosts the conversation and helps everyone find space to take part. The discussion develops around the session topic and the experiences people choose to share, so there is room to explore different perspectives and ask questions.

You decide how much you would like to contribute. You can listen while you get to know the group, join the discussion when you feel ready or take a break whenever you need one.

campaignWhat might we talk about?

Topics come from the questions people encounter in solidarity and community work. We might explore how a group works through disagreement or what helps new people feel welcome. Another conversation could consider how people stay involved when the work becomes demanding.

NAJMA / 04

English in the Café

English allows people from different language backgrounds to join the same conversation. Intermediate English, around B1 or above, is recommended: you should be able to follow the main points of a discussion and explain your ideas.

The Café offers informal conversation about solidarity and social change. If you would like English teaching with individual language feedback, you can explore Najma’s private lessons.

NAJMA / 05

Practical questions

Do I have to speak?add

You are welcome to listen throughout a session or join in when you feel ready. There is space for both ways of taking part.

Is the Café therapy?add

The Café is an informal community discussion, with the facilitator helping people take part. Counselling and psychotherapy fall outside the scope of the sessions.

Are sessions recorded?add

Sessions are usually unrecorded. Any exception requires clear agreement from everyone taking part.

Can I ask for an access adjustment?add

You can tell us about access needs when you register, or email info@najmacollective.org beforehand. Each event listing will explain the access options available for that session.
