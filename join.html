For teachers

Bring your expertise. Keep your professional voice.

Najma begins with a small international group of highly qualified teachers. Each teacher chooses their methods, materials, meeting platform, rate and availability. Najma provides one shared place for learners to find you and book.

The founding group will decide together how much shared infrastructure genuinely helps learners. Any common system should protect continuity while leaving teachers free to teach well.
